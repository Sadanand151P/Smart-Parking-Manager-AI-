HOW IT WORKS

Find → Predict → Book → Park

1. Discover

See every registered parking facility near your destination, filtered by price, EV charging, or covered parking.

2. AI Recommendation

Our model predicts availability at your arrival time and scores each option on distance, price, and predicted space.

3. Pre-Book

Reserve your slot in advance — no more arriving to a full lot. Pay by UPI, card, or cash on arrival.

4. Park with a QR Ticket

Scan in at the entrance with your digital ticket. SMS reminders keep you on schedule.
